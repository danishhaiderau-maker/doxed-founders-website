This phone needs an update

Doxxed Crypto loads our live site inside the app. Your Android System WebView is too old, which causes a white screen on Android 6 and similar devices.

Try these steps (in order):

  1. Open Play Store → search Android System WebView → tap Update (also update Google Chrome).
  2. Reboot the phone, then open this app again.
  3. If it still fails, use Chrome and go to doxxedcrypto.digital (works best on Android 8+).
  4. Recommended: use a phone on Android 8 or newer for the APK.

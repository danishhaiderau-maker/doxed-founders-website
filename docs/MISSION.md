# Mission — DoxxedCrypto.digital

**Live site:** [doxxedcrypto.digital](https://doxxedcrypto.digital)  
**What we are:** A curated intelligence and trust layer for crypto — built for retail who care about tech, and for founders who ship in public.

---

## Why this platform exists

Crypto retail is drowning in noise. Anonymous founders, paid influencers, and meme-driven pumps have trained people to chase liquidity instead of conviction. Scam teams extract value and leave; honest builders get buried in the same feed as the next shitcoin.

**DoxxedCrypto.digital exists to reverse that.**

We surface **legitimate projects with public founders** — teams that document, ship, and stand behind their work. We give retail a place to discover **tech and execution**, not hype cycles. We give founders a place to **build in public**, earn reputation, and connect with people who fund substance over spectacle.

This is not anti-meme or anti-retail speculation. It is **pro-accountability, pro-transparency, and pro-builder**. We believe investing in crypto can be relevant again when it is tied to people who actually build.

## Founder OS — your AI-assisted control plane

**Founder OS** is the web command center for crypto-native founders:

- **Copilot** — context-aware assistant grounded in your repo, tasks, and vault metadata.
- **Build queue** — ideas, specs, GitHub issues, and roadmap in one flow.
- **Integrations** — GitHub, Vercel, Railway deploy webhooks → suggested updates when you ship.
- **Publish everywhere** — translate build activity into founder updates and social-ready content.
- **Privacy stack** — choose how much lives in cloud vs on your machine (see [PRIVACY_STACK.md](./PRIVACY_STACK.md)).

Founder OS orchestrates; it does not replace your judgment. You stay in control of what is public and what stays private.

---

## Founder Node — self-custody for founder memory

**Founder Node** is a desktop app (Windows / macOS) that pairs with Founder OS:

- Stores **project memory** locally (`~/FounderVault/`): goals, roadmap, tasks, decisions, private notes.
- **Encrypts** sensitive payloads before any cloud relay — the API sees metadata, not plaintext.
- Runs **local Ollama** jobs so Copilot can answer without sending prompts to a cloud LLM.
- **Vector index (v2)** for semantic search over your vault on your hardware.

Founder Node is for founders who want **AI help without surrendering company intelligence**.

## Bring your own keys (BYOK)

We do not lock you into one AI vendor.

- **Bring Your Own AI** — OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, or local Ollama ([BYO_AI.md](./BYO_AI.md)).
- **Bring your own exchange keys** — for hired trading agents and showcase bots (encrypted at rest; never logged).
- **Bring your own GitHub** — Founder OS syncs from repos you authorize.

You choose providers. You pay vendors directly. The platform wires orchestration — not surveillance.

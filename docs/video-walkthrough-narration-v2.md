## Chapter 1 — Welcome & Landing (0:00–0:18)

Welcome to DoxxedCrypto — the platform where founders build in public and earn trust before they launch. Your laptop is the compute. Founder OS connects your IDE, your vault, and cloud brains in one workspace. Let us walk through everything you need to get started.

## Chapter 2 — Sign Up / Sign In (0:18–0:45)

Signing in takes seconds. Use **Continue with X** if you are a builder who wants the fast track — that unlocks **Verified Builder** status. Use email if you are here to trade, scout, or vote.

Expand **How free AI tokens work on Founder OS** to see the two tiers. **Verified Builder** gives you five hundred thousand tokens per day for thirty days, plus free DDollar and GLM five point two for ninety days — verify your X handle and connect GitHub and Cursor when you are ready. **Trial** is email-only: twenty-five thousand tokens per day to explore the product, with bring-your-own-key anytime. Real builders get the fast track — and upgrading is always one X verification and one commit away.

---

## Chapter 3 — Founder OS Paths (0:45–1:00)

Founder OS gives you three ways to run. **Sovereign** keeps everything on your machine — maximum privacy, lowest cost. **Hybrid** blends local compute with cloud models when you need them. **Founder Cloud** scales to hosted infrastructure once your idea is ready for the world.

---

## Chapter 4 — Download Founder Node (1:00–1:22)

Head to the sovereign onboarding hub at doxxedcrypto dot digital slash founder-den — not GitHub releases. Download Founder Node for your operating system right here. Install it, launch from your tray, and keep it running. Founder Node pairs with a single code, sends a heartbeat every forty-five seconds, and can run Ollama locally so private notes never leave your laptop. No remote desktop, no VPN, no open ports.

---

## Chapter 5 — Pair in Settings (1:22–1:38)

Once you are signed in, open **Settings** in Founder OS. Under **Pair your device**, choose Founder Vault — Founder Node — and click **Code for desktop**. Copy that code into the tray app under **Pair with Founder OS**. When the status turns green, your vault syncs and your IDE connection is live.

---

## Chapter 6 — Development Workspace & Cursor Agent (1:38–2:08)

Open the **Development Workspace**. If Founder Node is online, your Cursor workspaces appear in the sidebar automatically. Select a Cursor chat session, type your task in the Founder OS chat box — for example, “Add a health check endpoint to my API” — and send it. Founder OS dispatches to Cursor and the agent activates remotely. Progress streams right here in the browser. You do not need to context-switch back to the IDE unless you want to.
